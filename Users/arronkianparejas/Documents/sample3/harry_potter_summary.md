# Harry Potter Summary

Harry Potter is a series of seven fantasy novels written by British author J.K. Rowling. The books follow the life of a young wizard named Harry Potter, who discovers he has magical abilities and attends Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry.

### Book 1: Harry Potter and the Sorcerer's Stone
- **Plot**: Harry is an orphan living with his cruel aunt and uncle. On his 11th birthday, he receives a letter from Hogwarts inviting him to attend as a student. He discovers he is a wizard and learns about his parents' deaths at the hands of Lord Voldemort.
- **Characters**: Harry Potter, Ron Weasley, Hermione Granger

### Book 2: Harry Potter and the Chamber of Secrets
- **Plot**: At Hogwarts, Harry learns that an old secret chamber exists in the school. He discovers he is a descendant of Salazar Slytherin and must confront the dark wizard who sealed the chamber.
- **Characters**: Harry Potter, Ron Weasley, Hermione Granger

### Book 3: Harry Potter and the Prisoner of Azkaban
- **Plot**: Harry learns about the Dementors, creatures that feed on happiness. He faces challenges at Hogwarts, including a break-in by Sirius Black, who is after the Philosopher's Stone.
- **Characters**: Harry Potter, Ron Weasley, Hermione Granger

### Book 4: Harry Potter and the Goblet of Fire
- **Plot**: Harry participates in the Triwizard Tournament, a dangerous competition between three schools. He faces challenges from other students and learns more about his past.
- **Characters**: Harry Potter, Ron Weasley, Hermione Granger

### Book 5: Harry Potter and the Order of the Phoenix
- **Plot**: Harry discovers that Lord Voldemort has returned and is planning to destroy the wizarding world. He joins the Order of the Phoenix, a group fighting against Voldemort's forces.
- **Characters**: Harry Potter, Ron Weasley, Hermione Granger

### Book 6: Harry Potter and the Half-Blood Prince
- **Plot**: Harry learns about his parentage and the Horcruxes, objects that contain a piece of Voldemort's soul. He faces challenges at Hogwarts and prepares for the final battle.
- **Characters**: Harry Potter, Ron Weasley, Hermione Granger

### Book 7: Harry Potter and the Deathly Hallows
- **Plot**: The final book follows Harry, Ron, and Hermione as they fight against Voldemort in a series of battles. They uncover the secrets behind the Hallows and confront Voldemort for the last time.
- **Characters**: Harry Potter, Ron Weasley, Hermione Granger

Harry Potter has become one of the most beloved and successful fantasy series of all time, with its rich characters, imaginative world, and timeless themes.
